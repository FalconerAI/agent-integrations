# Falconer for ChatGPT and Codex

Search, read, create, and update Falconer documents from ChatGPT and Codex.
Connect your Falconer account when prompted and choose the workspace you want to use.
Access follows the permissions of your connected account.

The included Falconer skill provides guidance for working with documents.
