# Privacy

Falconer Agent Integrations connects supported AI agent clients to Falconer through the hosted MCP endpoint at `https://falconer.com/api/mcp`.

When you use this integration, your agent client may send MCP requests and receive Falconer tool results. Falconer handles those requests according to Falconer's privacy and security practices. Your AI agent client may also process prompts, tool calls, and tool results according to that client's own terms and privacy policy.

Do not place credentials, OAuth tokens, local configuration, customer content, organization IDs, document IDs, or private screenshots in this repository.

For Falconer's current privacy policy, visit [falconer.com](https://falconer.com).
